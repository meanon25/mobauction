# MobAuction 0.6 acceptance checks

Run on WoW Forever with Lua errors enabled. These checks have not yet been executed in this workspace.

1. Close WoW, run install.sh, restart. Confirm MobAuction 0.6.0 appears once in AddOns with its icon and inside `/moba`. The old ForgeProfit folder should only exist under backups.
2. Log into a character without MobAuction setup. Confirm the setup window opens. Click Detect my professions; compare primary and secondary professions and ranks with the Skills pane. Save, reload, and confirm selections persist. `/moba` and `/mobauction` open MobAuction; `/ma` stays available to other addons.
3. On Forever with the legacy profession window, open Cooking and Blacksmithing, expand all recipe groups, and let the list load without clicking Refresh or typing a command. Confirm the imported count increases and Full AH scan requests a complete market snapshot. Also test an empty import: a full market scan must still run. Open each crafting profession. Check Catalogue for known and unlearned recipes and unsupported non-item outputs. Compare reagent counts and minimum yield for three recipes with Blizzard's window. Switch to another character and verify setup is separate.
4. At a profession trainer, verify at least one imported unlearned recipe's minimum rank, training price and availability. Check that a recipe above selected skill does not enter Worth Learning. Reopen the profession and ensure trainer data survives refresh.
5. At a supplier, record a reagent with a bundle size greater than one. Compare the cost for a quantity crossing a bundle boundary with actual purchase cost.
6. At the AH, finish Full AH scan. Open a recipe's batch check. Check each reagent; request more results if needed. For a commodity spanning two prices, compare the full-batch cost with the purchase panel. For noncommodities, compare with whole buyout stacks, not unit fractions. Include own auctions and bid-only auctions in the comparison.
7. Verify that incomplete results, insufficient stock and data older than 60 seconds cannot produce a checked cost. Purchasing, closing/reopening the AH, posting and cancelling should clear detailed quotes. Inspecting another item must not assign its price book to the previous item.
8. Compare an output's deposit with the posting panel for each duration. Without a quote, profit after failures remains unknown. Verify a manual per-unit deposit override and its removal.
9. Check Worth Learning's one-craft profit against a profitable unlearned trainer recipe. Confirm the Learn price is displayed separately, never deducted from profit, and changing that price alone cannot change ranking. Check missing prices, unavailable training and an unsupported training cost are excluded. A displayed sale profit is still an estimate.
10. Browse recipe items with AHledger enabled. Compare a candidate against its displayed 7-day/30-day median. Repeat the browse update and verify no duplicate alert for 30 minutes. A price rise should remove a stale candidate. Disable alerts and verify silence. Items without historical data must not trigger an alert.
11. Open Catalogue: the main page must disappear. Click a recipe: Catalogue must disappear. Back/Escape must return through Catalogue to the main page. Test twenty-row pagination, scrolling, setup, Fees & settings and Help & status; no MobAuction text should show through another page. Verify the version says 0.6.0. Invalid settings must not partially save. Closing a batch check must refresh the main profits.

12. Enable Questie and QuestieDB. Open `/moba farms`. Confirm the detected character level, manual level override, Detect reset, paging, and tab switching. Compare selected NPC minimum levels with the actual creatures. Raising/lowering the filter must change the selected source or exclude the item; it must not keep a higher-level source's rate on a lower-level source.
13. At the AH, click Scan farm prices. Let Auctionator finish, then return and Refresh. Confirm current item values, cut rounding, reference-rate sorting and the model per 100 kills. Stale prices must disappear. Missing item names should request a retry rather than silently skipping items.
14. Click a cloth, cooking ingredient and elemental material. Compare creature names, zones, coordinates and reference percentages with Questie. Confirm the source provenance and Forever uncertainty are visible. Check real NPC rank/faction and outdoor location; rare/elite/friendly/instance sources must not be suggested as normal solo farms. Travel to sample locations to check the provider's coordinates on Forever.
15. Switch All levels + unknown. Confirm higher-level mobs are marked and gathering objects display unknown minimum level, gathering skill and rate. Unknown rates must never display a fabricated percentage or gold/hour. Details must show all applicable alternate source levels, including above-filter warnings.
16. Disable Questie/QuestieDB and reload. Recommended Farms should explain the missing dependency, with crafting still working. With providers enabled but unloaded/missing rate data, known locations should remain usable with unknown rates. Verify no Lua errors during first login/provider initialization.
17. Compare the single-item deposit in farm details with the game quote; verify values remain unknown without a quote. Check that the tab clearly labels its ranking as before deposits and expenses, and that failed-listing loss uses configured failures. No scan, tab or item click should buy, post, train or travel automatically.

18. Upgrade a character with older modern imports. Confirm repair guidance appears instead of treating old Fishing-labelled Cooking recipes as craftable. Open Cooking and let its list load, then open Blacksmithing and let its list load. Both imports must happen automatically. Close both windows and select Cooking only in MobAuction: its known recipes must remain visible. Switch filters, open/close Fishing, use Refresh and scan at the AH; neither profession's recipes should be reassigned or lost. Reopen Cooking, reload and verify the corrected imports persist.
19. With detected Cooking 244 and a manual 225 preview, confirm both ranks are shown. Click Use detected levels; known recipes observed at 244 should appear. Gain a skill point and check that automatic tracking follows it. Test the same action in Character setup; unselected professions and undetected manual ranks must not be cleared. Repeat with a deliberate lower preview to verify it still filters recipes.

20. With Cooking imports present and no Blacksmithing imports, select Blacksmithing. Confirm the first count says 0 plans, with the global catalogue total shown separately. Open WoW's Blacksmithing crafting list and wait for automatic importing; do not click Refresh. Confirm plan counts, Plan heading, plan detail ID, catalogue title and Next/Previous wording. Switch to Cooking and All my professions and check wording changes correctly. During a profession switch, loading feedback should clear when the game is ready; one incomplete plan must not hide other validated plans.

21. With an eligible imported recipe/plan, open the AH, then `/moba` and Scan AH. It must recognize the open window and start Auctionator searches. Repeat after closing/reopening the AH, and after a reload (reopen the AH if WoW closes it). Test a reagent Check, a deposit quote and Scan farm prices. Close the AH and retry: requests must be refused and previous detailed quotes/deals must not be reused on the next visit. Opening a bank/vendor or merely opening the AH must not initiate a scan. Help & status should agree with the auction window's visibility.

22. In Crafting, compare rows with different sale profits, cost confidence and deposit amounts. Sort must follow Sale profit descending; a checked lower profit cannot outrank a higher estimate. Unknown profits come after known losses. Change batch size and verify recalculation.
23. Open a profession while its list is loading, switch professions quickly, and close during loading. Pending imports must settle automatically or report why they stopped; closing must not import stale data. Check delayed reagent names and newly learned plans. No repeated chat spam or endless polling.
24. In Worth Learning at the AH, click Scan recipes and More recipes. Check exact recipe-to-craft identification against the actual item tooltip. Already-known recipes, other professions and known above-skill recipes must not be recommended. An unknown mapping must be explained. Test a higher manual skill preview: it must not make an unusable item learnable. Change the AH browse search and confirm More recipes does not page someone else's query.
25. Check an auctioned recipe's reagents, output price and deposit. Compare Profit / craft with one craft's output revenue minus cut, ingredients and expected failed deposits. Learn price must be displayed separately and never change that profit, ranking or flag. Change the normal Crafting batch to seven and verify Worth Learning and its detail view still use one craft. The recipe purchase Check must query the recipe item, not its crafted output.
26. A Worth learning flag needs checked positive crafting profit, current usability and fresh AHledger estimated demand of at least one output unit/day. Missing demand can show a profit candidate, not a guaranteed sale. Missing deposits or stock must show needs-check feedback. Let a recipe listing age past five minutes, learn it, or replace it with your own listing and verify its candidate disappears; close/reopen the AH and verify fresh observations are required.
27. Quote a commodity deposit without owning it, then an equipment output with one copy in bags. Compare short/medium/long durations and two quantities with the posting panel. Move or craft an output and verify bag updates refresh the quote. Missing quotes must remain unknown; a real zero is valid. Check no long learning status overlaps the main controls or bottom labels.

Record build, addon version shown inside the window, recipe/item IDs, expected values, observed values and any Lua stack trace for failures. API mocks do not substitute for these checks. After saving/reloading, `MobAuctionDB.loadedVersion` and `lastImportAttempt` identify the initialized version and the latest active import attempt.

28. Reproduce Savory Stag Sliders with two Stag Meat at 70 copper and three spices (item 2692; use the current client name). Confirm full vendor bundle cost, 5% cut, and assumed 1s per output item. Missing spice prices must remain unknown. Wheel-scroll across more than ten results and verify Sale profit descends across page boundaries in both modes.
29. Start Full AH scan once and keep the AH open. Verify a completion count, both recipe and ingredient prices, and the cooldown on a second click. Start a scan in Auctionator and confirm MobAuction accepts the shared server response. Close the AH during loading; old complete prices should remain, with cancellation shown. Temporarily unavailable item names must not block valid instant item information.
30. Install and enable Recipe Registry's Forever edition. Open Catalogue and Worth Learning with no unlearned imports. Database research should appear with unknown learned status and explicit reference evidence; unsupported and removed records should be excluded. Open professions and confirm current client data supersedes database entries. Do not mark a static database entry confirmed learnable.
31. In Worth Learning, toggle Learning: all sources / Learning: AH recipes. Check a listed recipe/plan with cheap materials; its whole-listing purchase price must be separate from sale profit. Change only its recipe-item asking price and verify craft profit/ranking stay the same. Ingredient checks must still identify shortages and full-stack costs.
32. Check complete snapshot absence: remove a material's buyout listings and rescan after the cooldown. Old Auctionator prices must not make the absent material available in that snapshot. The item can reappear after a later full scan. Snapshot prices remain forecasts away from the AH.
